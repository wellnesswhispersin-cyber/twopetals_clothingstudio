Our Story

Two Sisters.
One Dream.
Two Petals.

It started with two sisters dreaming together. Dreaming about clothes. Dreaming about a space of their own. Dreaming about creating something that felt beautiful, personal and completely theirs.

Then came the hard part — turning the dream into reality. Empty walls became a studio. Late-night conversations became plans. Small decisions became big milestones. And every challenge became another reason to keep going.

Today, Two Petals is more than a clothing studio. It is a piece of that dream, brought to life.

Built together. Worn by you.
— The Two Petals Sisters
